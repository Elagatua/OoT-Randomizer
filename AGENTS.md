## Merge Conflicts
- Files under `ASM/build/` and `data/generated/` are compiled/generated artifacts (e.g. `bundle.o`, `*_symbols.txt`, `symbols.json`, `patch_symbols.json`, `rom_patch.txt`).
- When resolving merge conflicts, conflicts in these two folders can simply be reverted (pick either side) — they are regenerated by the ASM compiler. Recompile through the ASM build after all other (hand-written source) conflicts are resolved, then commit the freshly generated artifacts.
